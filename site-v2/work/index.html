Selected Work

Four programs.
Three continents.

Each one was built from a different brief. Each one was judged against the same standard — that the room is still serving the menu well a year after opening night.

Nicosia, Cyprus  ·  2014 – 2022

Lost & Found Drinkery

Head bartender. Founding bar team.

A twenty-seat bar tucked off a side street in old Nicosia. When the bar opened, Cypriot cocktail culture meant vodka and Red Bull. By 2015 it was on the World’s 50 Best Bars list — the first Cypriot bar ever to make it. In 2018 it ranked #25 and earned two Tales of the Cocktail Spirited Awards Top 10 nominations the same year: Best International Cocktail Bar and Best International Bar Team. It held a place on the list for nine consecutive years.

The program was built on a tight repertoire, technique-led prep, and a team trained to call regulars by name before they reached the stool. The peer set in the 2018 Tales cohort included Manhattan Bar Singapore, the Connaught, the Savoy’s American Bar, Tippling Club, Speak Low Shanghai, and the Clumsies Athens. Lost & Found held its own.

Recognition

  • World’s 50 Best Bars #25, 2018 — first Cypriot bar on the list
  • World’s 50 Best Bars 2015 – 2023 (nine consecutive years across main and extended lists)
  • Tales of the Cocktail Spirited Awards 2018 — Best International Cocktail Bar, Top 10
  • Tales of the Cocktail Spirited Awards 2018 — Best International Bar Team, Top 10
  • Featured in Difford’s Guide, Bargiornale, Parikiaki, and across European bar press
Nicosia, Cyprus  ·  2020 – 2022

Tocayo

Bar program and cocktail menu, concept through opening.

Tocayo is one of Nicosia’s most ambitious restaurants — a kitchen that runs on technique, sourcing, and quiet confidence. The bar had to keep up. Andreas built the program from scratch: a short, opinionated list paired to the food, with the seasonal turnover the chef expected from the kitchen also expected from the bar.

The bar list was deliberately small. No twenty-cocktail menus. Twelve drinks, every one of them defensible against the room. Service-volume considerations baked in from day one — every cocktail prepped, batched where the integrity allowed, plated within thirty seconds in service. The kind of thing operators learn the hard way after opening.

Recognition

  • Best New Restaurant Cyprus, 2021
  • Sustained press coverage in Cypriot food and lifestyle media
Austin, TX  ·  2022 – 2023

In Plain Sight

Bar program consulting.

In Plain Sight is a reservation-only, hidden-entry cocktail bar in downtown Austin — a tiny eight-seat room at 612 Brazos Street, run by bartender Conor O’Reilly. Andreas consulted on the bar program at a critical stage: refining the menu’s technical floor and helping the team build prep workflows for a venue where every drink would be visible to every guest.

Within twelve months of opening, In Plain Sight was on the Spirited Awards 2023 U.S. Regional Top 10 Honoree list for Best New U.S. Cocktail Bar, in the U.S. Central region — alongside Houston’s Refuge and EZ’s Liquor Lounge. The room has since become an Austin institution in the way that small, focused bars do — by being the place bartenders go on their nights off.

Recognition

  • Tales of the Cocktail Spirited Awards 2023 — U.S. Central Regional Top 10 Honoree, Best New U.S. Cocktail Bar
  • The Infatuation, Austin Chronicle, Eater Austin features
Abu Dhabi, UAE  ·  2019

Casa de Cuba

Hotel & restaurant beverage consulting.

A Caribbean-driven cocktail and rum program in a market that didn’t default to spirit-forward cocktails. Andreas built a list that reads as a single editorial — heavy on rum, aware of the desert climate, designed for service volume against an international guest base.

The program leaned on long-aged rums, smoked syrups, and tropical acids that could survive the temperature differential between the bar and the table. Training emphasized speed under volume — Friday nights in Abu Dhabi mean covers that NYC operators don’t typically forecast for.

Engagement

  • Full opening program — concept, menu, supplier setup, team training, opening-week support
  • Reference point for subsequent Mediterranean and Middle Eastern engagements

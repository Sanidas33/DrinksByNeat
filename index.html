What we build

Four packages, scoped and priced. The flagship is the full opening program; the others stand alone or stack with it.

01  — flagship

Opening Beverage Program

Full opening engagement: program concept, menu, supplier relationships, prep-lab build, team training, opening-week support, ninety-day optimization.

From $55,000 + milestone payment at 90‑day review

02

Program Refresh

Six-to-ten week engagement for venues whose beverage program has gone stale or whose food has outgrown the bar. Audit, new menu, retrain.

$18,000 – $32,000

03

Prep Lab Design & Build

Equipment specification, room layout, SOPs, training, and 30-day post-launch support. Rotovaps, centrifuges, sous-vide, ultrasonic homogenizers, Spinzalls.

$12,000 – $28,000 + equipment pass-through

04

White-Label & RTD Development

Bottled-cocktail line for hotels, restaurant groups, and independent brands. Recipe, sourcing, co-packer introduction, creative direction.

$22,000 – $45,000 + per-unit royalty

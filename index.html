Tocayo

In Plain Sight

Cocktail and rum program for an Abu Dhabi venue trading on a deep Caribbean catalog. Built a list that reads as a single editorial — heavy on rum, aware of the climate, designed for service volume in a market that doesn’t default to spirit-forward cocktails.

03What we do

From classic builds
to molecular mixology.

01  Program

Programs, built from the brief.

  • Complete beverage program overhaul
  • Opening concept & positioning
  • Refresh of existing menus
  • Multi-unit program design
  • Seasonal & rotational updates
  • Wine and spirits curation
  • Non-alcoholic program

02  Menu & Recipes

From the classics to signatures.

  • Signature cocktail development
  • Classic & traditional builds
  • Recipe creation & costing
  • Ingredient sourcing & prep specs
  • House syrups, cordials, shrubs, tinctures
  • Elaborate & service-ready garnishes
  • NA & low-ABV variants

03  Technique

Basic to molecular — the full stack.

  • Fat & oil washing, milk wash clarification
  • Rotary evaporation, vacuum distillation
  • Centrifuge clarification, ultrasonic infusion
  • Sous-vide infusion & cooking
  • Carbonation, force-carb, soda design
  • Fermentation, koji, vinegar & shrubs
  • Hydrocolloids, foams, gels & spheres

04  Design & Build

Bars, prep labs, production lines.

  • Bar design & service-flow planning
  • Prep-lab build — layout & SOPs
  • Equipment specification & sourcing
  • HACCP-compliant production protocols
  • Glassware, tools, smallwares spec
  • Bottled cocktail & RTD production line
  • White-label co-packer relationships

05  Operations

Cost, efficiency, margin.

  • Pour-cost & margin optimization
  • Inventory systems & par-level setup
  • Supplier negotiation & account setup
  • Workflow audit & service-flow design
  • Batching & prep-ahead protocols
  • Waste reduction & yield management
  • Reporting & analytics workflows

06  Training & Service

Staff trained to run it.

  • Opening-team training, two weeks on-site
  • Technique transfer — basic to advanced
  • Service standards & pace
  • Menu memorization & storytelling
  • Hospitality & guest engagement
  • Ongoing technique masterclasses
  • Train-the-trainer for multi-unit teams

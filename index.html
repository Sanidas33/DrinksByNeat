03What we build

Four engagements.
Scoped, not templated.

01

Opening Beverage Program

12 — 16 weeks pre-opening

Full opening engagement: program concept, menu, supplier relationships, prep-lab build, team training, opening-week support, and ninety days of post-opening optimization. The flagship.

02

Program Refresh

6 — 10 weeks

For venues whose beverage program has gone stale or whose food has outgrown the bar. Audit, new menu, retrain. The Tuesday-night discipline of a Saturday-night room.

03

Prep‑Lab Design & Build

8 — 14 weeks

Equipment specification, room layout, SOPs, training, post-launch support. Rotovaps, centrifuges, sous-vide, ultrasonic homogenizers, Spinzalls — sized to throughput, not to a brochure.

04

White-Label & RTD Development

14 — 24 weeks

Bottled-cocktail line for hotels, restaurant groups, and independent brands. Recipe, sourcing, co-packer introduction, creative direction. The differentiator: a working production network already in place.
